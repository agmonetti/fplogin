# SDDM patch series

The series targets SDDM `v0.21.0` (`63780fcd79f1dbf81a30eef48c28c699ab15aded`). Apply it after the pinned Arch CMake compatibility change `228778c2b4b7e26db1e1d69fe484ed75c5791c3a`; this series does not include or replace that packaging change.

Apply in listed order with `git apply` from the SDDM source root. The patch adds request/method identity, deferred winner authorization, concurrent password/fingerprint helpers, greeter control IPC, peer-PID verification, bounded nonblocking helper framing, stale-result rejection and queued context replacement. PAM packaging and QML/theme behavior remain out of scope.

The patch includes the earlier five security-review corrections, the production winner gate, and a remediation for the helper socket-disconnect/SIGKILL race, destructor-time timers, and logind session reuse. `AuthAttemptGate` covers winner and cancellation order; `AuthTransport` includes a real `Auth`/socket exchange with a synthetic helper that disconnects after authorization and exits 0. A clean pinned patch applies; Qt 6 builds and CTest passes 6/6 in an isolated Arch container. A fresh QEMU guest with synthetic PAM observed winning-helper `0/normal` after `pam_end` and reactivated the same logind session without a duplicate graphical session. Physical-reader R01–R16 have **not** been rerun after remediation; Sprint 1 is **unaccepted** and Sprint 2 QML remains blocked. See `docs/reviews/sprint-1.md`.
